CV | Google Scholar


I am a Ph.D. candidate in the School of Electrical Engineering at KAIST, advised by Prof. Jae-Woong Jeong. I received my B.S. in Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering from KAIST in 2021 with a minor in Electrical Engineering (Cum Laude). From July to December 2024, I was a visiting researcher at North Carolina State University, working with Prof. Michael D. Dickey.

My research focuses on liquid-metal-based soft and stretchable electronics, variable-stiffness bioelectronics, and multifunctional sensors. I develop material platforms and fabrication strategies—such as phase-change metal inks, gallium microgranule composites, and thermally responsive liquid-metal systems—that enable transformative bioelectronic devices, adaptive electronic skins, and hardware security primitives like physical unclonable functions.
